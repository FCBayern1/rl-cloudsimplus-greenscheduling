# PREFREEZE_DIAGNOSTIC — not part of the frozen screen

This pre-screen touched DISCOVERY turbines before the Addendum was frozen, and it carried
a rho band of [0.2, 3.0] that had been chosen after seeing the design pilot. It is archived
here in full and excluded from the formal screen. Its output was not read.

Three logic errors were also found in it and are fixed in the frozen implementation:
absolute correlation admitted strong negative pairs, the simultaneous-low-wind test asked
whether any site lacked green rather than all three, and the "optimal DC never changes"
branch passed instead of rejecting.
