# Stage D-B preregistration: the credit-assignment baseline (CCA-PG) — FROZEN 2026-09-04

A separate preregistration so the four-line Stage D stays frozen. Everything not stated here is inherited from `STAGE_D_LONGRUN_PREREG.md` (frozen, §5 and §8): the HZ ×2 scene, the ledger-aligned reward, 400,000 steps per line, the five paired seeds 20260904–08, training on the six development windows and judging on the six unread windows, the primary corruption `calibrated_shrink_v1` with shuffle and anti as negative controls, the deployment protocol, the readers and the contract rules.

## 1. Why this baseline

EU-CRD is a credit-assignment method. Without a modern credit-assignment comparison the reader cannot tell how much of any effect is "some hindsight-aware credit assignment" and how much is EU-CRD's specific decomposition. CCA-PG (Mesnard et al., 2021) is the nearest prior work: it subtracts a learned hindsight baseline conditioned on a future statistic, which in this environment is the realised future green power, an exogenous, action-independent quantity. It is implemented in this repository (`drl-manager/src/learners/cca.py`, hooked in `crd_q_loss.py::_apply_cca`, unit tests `tests/test_cca.py`, `tests/test_cca_integration.py`) and was removed from an earlier draft on 2026-08-03; restoring it before submission was recorded as required at the time.

## 2. Lines

Two lines, identical to the frozen V line except for identity, the forecast channel and the credit-assignment block:

| line | forecast during training | credit assignment |
|---|---|---|
| N_C | future forecast hollowed (`forecast_mode: none`) | vanilla PPO backbone with `crd.cca.enabled: true` |
| C | clean truth-informed forecast | the same |

`crd.enabled` stays false, so the ensemble critic and the responsibility decomposition are off and the backbone is the vanilla one; the learner routes to the CCA hook. Hindsight parameters are the frozen defaults from the original integration: horizon 12, hidden 64, lr 1e-3, train_iters 1. Config `g1/compressed_timecap_s2/config_stage_d_cca.yml`, SHA256 f949eead3e3770e0932af5f9d9ea4d2467db994b648f43ba9e05ac6635c4c763, generated by `gen_stage_d.py 400000 cca` with the diff against V limited to identity, `forecast_mode` and `crd` by test.

## 3. Readings and gates

Per seed, final checkpoint, the six cells × six judgement windows, stochastic decode: C_NC (hollow), C_C0 (clean), C_C1 (calibrated shrink), plus shuffle and anti for reporting. Pooled carbon intensity as in the main preregistration.

- B1 the baseline uses the forecast: (C_NC − C_C0) / C_NC ≥ 5%.
- B2 the error hurts it: (C_C1 − C_C0) / C_C0 ≥ 5%.
- B3 comparison with EU-CRD, reported for the paper and not a pass/fail gate: the corruption increment (C_C1 − C_C0)/C_C0 against EU-CRD's (C_E1 − C_E0)/C_E0, and the clean levels C_C0 against C_E0.

Direction ≥ 4/5 seeds for B1 and B2. This preregistration produces a comparison, not a verdict on EU-CRD: the Stage D verdict is computed from the four frozen lines alone, whatever CCA-PG does. If CCA-PG turns out at least as robust as EU-CRD, that is reported as such in the same table.

## 4. Contract and validity

The rules of the main preregistration apply unchanged: a contract failure on a clean deployment is substantive, corrupted-deployment lateness stays in the analysis, missing files or a tier not in effect are INVALID, no (cell, window) is ever voided, and no result is read before all five seeds of this preregistration finish.

## 5. Execution

Ten single-GPU jobs on Isambard (five seeds × two lines) plus five dependent evaluation jobs, the same scripts as the main run with `CONFIG=config_stage_d_cca.yml`. The workstation is not used for this preregistration; it is finishing the main four lines.
