# Stage D-C preregistration: the risk-sensitive comparison set — FROZEN 2026-09-04

Approved by Codex on 2026-09-04 (R-y). A separate preregistration, so the four-line Stage D and the CCA-PG baseline both stay frozen. Everything not stated here is inherited from `STAGE_D_LONGRUN_PREREG.md`: the HZ ×2 scene, the ledger-aligned reward, 400,000 steps per line, the five paired seeds 20260904–08, the six development training windows and the six unread judgement windows, the deployment protocol, the readers and the contract rules.

## 1. Why

Without them the comparison set is no-forecast, vanilla, CCA-PG and EU-CRD, which is thin for a paper that claims a specific credit-assignment mechanism. Risk-sensitive objectives are the obvious alternative way to buy robustness to a bad input, and the earlier draft already compared against them on the old testbed; on the HZ scene they do not exist.

## 2. Lines

Four lines, each identical to the frozen V line except for identity and the risk objective. `crd.enabled` stays false, so the backbone, the observation and the training budget are V's; the learner routes to the risk-transformed advantage.

| line | objective | frozen parameters |
|---|---|---|
| RCV | CVaR | alpha 0.2, lam 0.5 |
| RRS | risk-sensitive exponential utility | beta 1.0 |
| RMV | mean-variance | lam 1.0 |
| RDC | distributional CVaR | alpha 0.1, lam 0.7 |

Parameters are the values the earlier campaign froze; **none is retuned for this scene**, and none may be retuned after any HZ number is seen. Config `g1/compressed_timecap_s2/config_stage_d_risk.yml`, SHA256 0a69f1fabd3f92acf6c272c0e32fb274a9e62ac256f354cf1067bfc51cd5e461, generated by `gen_stage_d.py 400000 risk`; a test asserts the diff against V is limited to identity, `forecast_mode` and the `crd` block.

**Matched no-forecast.** These four share the architecture and the observation space of V exactly, so they share **N_V** as their matched no-forecast line rather than each carrying its own, as the ruling permits. The test above is the evidence for "architecture and observations identical".

## 3. Readings and gates

Per seed, final checkpoint, six cells × six judgement windows, stochastic decode: clean and `calibrated_shrink_v1` for each line, as the ruling requires. shuffle and anti are not run for this set; the negative controls belong to the main preregistration.

- S1 the objective uses the forecast: (C_NV − C_X0) / C_NV ≥ 5%, per line X.
- S2 the error hurts it: (C_X1 − C_X0) / C_X0 ≥ 5%.
- S3 reported, not gated: the corruption increment (C_X1 − C_X0)/C_X0 for each risk line against EU-CRD's (C_E1 − C_E0)/C_E0, and the clean levels.

Direction ≥ 4/5 seeds for S1 and S2. **This preregistration produces a comparison, never a verdict**: Stage D's PASS or STOP is computed from its four frozen lines alone, whatever the risk objectives do. If a risk objective is more robust than EU-CRD, that is reported plainly in the same table.

## 4. Resources

Twenty single-GPU jobs (four lines × five seeds) plus five dependent evaluation jobs on Isambard, submitted only where they do not preempt the main experiment's resources: the workstation is untouched, and on the cluster these queue behind nothing that the main run needs, since the main run's twenty jobs are already resident. If the scheduler ever puts a risk job ahead of a main-run job, the risk job is cancelled and resubmitted later.

## 5. Contract and validity

As in the main preregistration: a contract failure on a clean deployment is substantive for that line, missing files or a tier not in effect are INVALID, no (cell, window) is ever voided, and no result is read before all five seeds of this preregistration finish.
